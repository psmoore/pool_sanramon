San Ramon Olympic Pool & Aquatic Park

A 50-meter Olympic pool with diving boards, a 25-yard lap pool and the Aquatic Park play pool, next to California High School. Lap swim, swim teams, Masters and aqua aerobics, year-round.

The pools

Olympic Pool

  • 50 meters long course, or 25 yards short course
  • 4 to 12 feet deep · 680,000 gallons · 80°F
  • Two 3-meter and two 1-meter diving boards
  • Colorado Time Systems scoreboard for meets

Lap Pool

  • 25 yards · 3.5 to 6 feet deep
  • 187,000 gallons · 82°F
  • Behind the picnic area, to the left of the lobby

Aquatic Park

  • Play pool with a new play structure · zero-depth entry to 1.5 feet
  • Home of the Splash N' Dash inflatable obstacle course
  • Recreation swim, May to September

Full-service locker rooms with showers, lockers (bring a lock) and suit spinners, and a family changing room. Every pool has easy-entry stairs, a swim lift and water wheelchairs. Established 1975; the aquatic center building was completed in 1999.
