Classes & Camps

Aqua aerobics, CPR and lifeguard courses, and summer camps at the pools. Days and times below come from the live schedule; register at SanRamonRecGuide.com.

Aqua Aerobics

A total-body workout set to music: the water's buoyancy is easy on joints, and its resistance builds strength. Shallow-water classes are low impact, and no swimming experience is needed.

CPR, First Aid, Lifeguard & Water Safety Instructor

Community CPR/AED and First Aid (ages 12 and up, $80 residents / $100 non-residents) runs at the Adams Safety Training Center, with 2027 dates coming soon. Lifeguard, Lifeguard Instructor and Water Safety Instructor courses are listed under Training & Employment.
