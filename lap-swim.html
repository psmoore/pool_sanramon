Lap Swim

Year-round lap swimming in pools heated to 80 degrees. Beginners to experts are welcome, and you can come any time during lap swim hours. Lanes are usually available for water walking, but not guaranteed.

Before you swim

  • Lap lanes are for exercise only; no recreational swimming, horseplay, coaching or lessons.
  • Children 12 and under swim with an adult or guardian watching; depending on ability, a chaperone may need to be in the water.
  • Share a lane when it's busy: split it with one other swimmer, or circle swim with more, keeping to the right.
  • If others are waiting, keep to 30 minutes or invite them to share.
  • Kickboards are provided; bring your own goggles, pull buoys and fins.
