Aquatics

The City of San Ramon runs two outdoor aquatic centers, both heated to 80–82 degrees year-round. Every time on these pages comes from the pools' live schedule, so when the City changes it, these pages change with it.

Where can I swim right now?

The aquatic centers

San Ramon Olympic Pool & Aquatic Park

9900 Broadmoor Dr, San Ramon, CA 94583 · (925) 973-3240

A 50-meter Olympic pool with diving boards, a 25-yard lap pool and the Aquatic Park play pool, next to California High School. Lap swim, swim teams, Masters and aqua aerobics, year-round.

…

Dougherty Valley Aquatic Center

10550 Albion Rd, San Ramon, CA 94582 · (925) 973-3335

A 50-meter Olympic pool with diving boards and a water play pool for small children, next to Dougherty Valley High School. Opened in 2007.

…

Who can swim

Lap swim is drop-in: pay at the front desk with a swim pass, card or cash. One All Swim Pass covers lap swim, water walking and, in summer, recreation swim at either center. See Lap Swim for prices.

What this replaces

  • Lap swim hours in a picture. The fall hours are a flyer image on the Lap Swim page, readable only by eye and only until it is replaced. Here they are text that updates itself.
  • An empty Aquatics Calendar. The City's aquatics calendar shows no events at all. Here every lap swim, class, practice and closure is on one calendar.
  • Teams with no times. Teams & Other Services lists eleven rental groups but none of their practice times; those live on each club's own website. Here practices sit beside lap swim, pool by pool.
  • Closures in a separate list. Holidays and special events are on their own page. Here a closure also clears that day from every schedule on the site.
